Our story

More than a restaurant

The Big Tree describes itself as more than a restaurant: a place to celebrate. In Gurugram, that means a social hotspot on Golf Course Road with a welcoming ambience, live music and food made for sharing.

From relaxed meals to lively evenings, birthdays and romantic dinners, the idea is simple: gather well, and make the moment memorable.
